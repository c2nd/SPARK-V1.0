Last updated: November 2026

At SPARK (accessible from c2nd.github.io/SPARK-V1.0), the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by SPARK and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

SPARK is a static website. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) unless you voluntarily contact us.

However, like most websites, we may collect non-personal information automatically, including:

  • Log Data: Your IP address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and the number of clicks. This is used to analyze trends and administer the site.
  • Cookies and Web Beacons: We may use cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and to optimize the user experience based on browser type and other information.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We may use third-party services and advertising networks to support the maintenance of this website. These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information for analytics and advertising purposes.

  • Analytics: We use services like Google Analytics (via Google Search Console) to understand how visitors interact with the site.
  • Advertising: Third-party ad networks (such as Adsterra, Monetag, or others) may serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet. These networks may use cookies to personalize ads.

Please note that SPARK has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

SPARK's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

4. Your Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following (depending on your jurisdiction, such as GDPR or CCPA):

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data (if any is held).
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. SPARK does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via Twitter or through the contact links provided on our website.
