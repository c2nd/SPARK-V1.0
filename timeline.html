HISTORY OF CYBER WARFARE

1988

Morris Worm

The first major internet worm, created by Robert Tappan Morris, accidentally crashes thousands of computers and brings the early internet to a halt.

1995

The Capture of Kevin Mitnick

The FBI arrests Mitnick, the most wanted hacker in the U.S., for stealing software and wire fraud. He becomes a symbol of early hacker culture.

1999

c0mrade Breaches NASA

15-year-old Jonathan James hacks into NASA and the DoD, stealing ISS software.

2010

Stuxnet Discovered

A malicious computer worm is discovered targeting Iran's nuclear program. It is considered the first known cyberweapon designed to cause physical damage.

2017

WannaCry Ransomware

A global ransomware attack encrypts hundreds of thousands of computers in over 150 countries, crippling hospitals and businesses.

2021

Colonial Pipeline Attack

The largest cyberattack on U.S. oil infrastructure forces the shutdown of a major pipeline, causing fuel shortages across the East Coast.
